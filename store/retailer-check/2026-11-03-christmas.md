# Christmas returns — 3 November 2026

Each shop's own returns page, read for the Christmas 2026 extension it
publishes. Every page below was read twice, independently: once through a web
fetch tool and once by fetching the page and searching its text (or, where the
shop refused that, a second web fetch). Both reads agree on every date that
went into `src/lib/christmas.ts`. Where a quote could only be taken through the
web fetch tool it is marked, because that text was not checked character for
character against the raw page.

Left out, and why:
- Karen Millen — "Any orders made between 30th October – 15th December can be
  returned until 5th Jan". No year anywhere in the sentence, and the two reads
  disagreed about whether it is this year's.
- Debenhams — the page gives two different end dates (none, and 15 December),
  and the item "must be received by us on or before 5 January", so the last
  day to SEND it is earlier than the date the app would show.
- Overclockers UK — "Saturday 1st November … Monday 23rd December": 1 November
  2026 is a Sunday. Last season's text.
- John Lewis — no Christmas wording on either page this time.
- Seasalt — in, but its last day is 19 December: the page says purchases from
  20 December "fall into our normal 28 days Returns policy" (28 days from the
  20th is the 17th of January either way).
